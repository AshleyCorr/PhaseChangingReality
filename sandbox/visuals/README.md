# Visuals (Ashley's study aids)

Pictures Ashley uses to understand Emily's ideas. They are **not** Emily's work and **not** statements of the
hypothesis: `HYPOTHESIS.md` is the claim, in her words. Where a visual and `HYPOTHESIS.md` differ, `HYPOTHESIS.md`
is right. Nothing here is a look, a test or a result, and nothing here has been checked by a physicist.

- `2026-11-10_big_unfolding_simulator.html` (10 November 2026). One self-contained page, opened in any browser:
  an interactive, narrated zoom in eight scales, from inside X out to the doughnut. Every idea on it carries a tag:
  **Emily** (simplified from `HYPOTHESIS.md` as of 6 November 2026), **Ashley** (his own additions: X pictured as a
  crystal, frozen time between the pairs, superposition, the Hawking partner as the spark, flaws making the burp
  uneven, uneven expansion, magnetism, a lopsided afterglow as a test, the new X tugging on its parent), **known
  physics**, or **open question**. The narration is generated with the open-source Kokoro voice model and is
  embedded in the file. Drafted with Claude (Anthropic) in conversation with Ashley.
