# Prior-work search: the surplus kind alternates from one burp to the next, set by which side of the boundary a foot is on

CLAUDE.md rule 15. Her rule of 6 November 2026 (small hours; `sandbox/NOTEBOOK.md` the same night): the two feet of
one seed carry opposite kinds, the outside foot is the daughter's surplus, and the inside foot carries the kind the
previous burp's outside foot carried, so successive burps alternate the surplus.

## What came closest

- **Hajdukovic** (CERN proceedings 2014; the 2025 arXiv paper 2501.08368 restates it): a cyclic universe whose cycles
  are alternately dominated by matter and by antimatter, each cycle's dominance setting the next. Abstract and press
  only. It rests on antimatter having gravitational charge of the opposite sign (antimatter falls up). That premise
  was measured against in 2023: the ALPHA-g experiment at CERN saw antihydrogen fall down under gravity like
  hydrogen (Anderson et al., Nature 621, 716, 2023; general knowledge, to verify). Her rule needs no antigravity:
  the sign is set by which side of the boundary a foot grows on. So the nearest published form of alternation is
  dead on its own premise and hers is a different mechanism; it is not already done.
- **Poplawski 2011** (arXiv:1101.4012; abstract and summary only): torsion in Einstein-Cartan gravity gives fermions
  and antifermions different energy levels at very high density, so decays favour matter; the antimatter partners
  go to a hidden sector that is the dark matter, total baryon number zero. One sign per bounce, set by the sign of
  the torsion coupling; no alternation. Closest in machinery (twist deciding the kind, inside a black-hole bounce).
  To be read in full before any citation.
- **Boyle, Finn and Turok 2018** (PRL 121, 251301; press and repository record): a universe and its CPT image on the
  two sides of the bang, books balanced. One pair, no cycle, no alternation. The shape of her twins at one burp.
- **Emergent cyclic inflation** (arXiv:1306.6927, abstract): a little asymmetry made at each cycle as the universe
  leaves equilibrium; a size that accumulates, not a sign that flips.

## Verdict

**FOUND, as a near miss**: alternation across cycles exists in the literature once (Hajdukovic), on a premise
measured false, with a different mechanism. The notebook may say "proposed once, on a premise since measured
against; her form differs", never "nobody has proposed alternation". The three nearest papers are to be read in
full before any is cited in a public page.

What would change the verdict: an arXiv full-text and INSPIRE search on "alternating baryon asymmetry" and "cyclic
baryogenesis sign"; the forward citations of Hajdukovic and of Poplawski 2011; reading Poplawski 2011 in full to see
whether successive bounces can flip the torsion's sign.
